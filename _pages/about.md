I am currently a first year PhD student working on Generative Models applied to Remote Sensing at [CESBIO](https://www.cesbio.cnrs.fr/) and [ISAE-SUPAERO](https://www.isae-supaero.fr/) under the supervision of [Mathieu Fauvel](https://www.cesbio.cnrs.fr/author/mathieu-fauvel/) and [Thomas Oberlin](https://pagespro.isae-supaero.fr/thomas-oberlin/). My current research work is in partnership with [RELEO](https://aniti.univ-toulouse.fr/fr_fr/representation-learning-for-earth-observation/) project.

Before starting my PhD adventure I worked as a Research Engineer at [INRIA Grenoble](https://team.inria.fr/robotlearn/) on Deep Reinforcement Learning applied to Robot Navigation in the [RobotLearn](https://team.inria.fr/robotlearn/) under the supervision of [Xavier Alameda-Pineda](https://xavirema.eu/) and [Chris Reinke](https://www.chris-reinke.com/home). Then I worked as a Research Engineer too at [CNRM - Meteo France](https://www.umr-cnrm.fr/) on Generative Models applied to Ensemble Weather Forecasting in the Previsibility Team under the supervision of [Laure Raynaud](https://cnrm.sedoo.fr/author/raynaudl/).

Research interests after I started my PhD:

- Generative Models (Diffusion Models, Flow Matching, Flow Maps)
- Multimodal Generative Models for Remote Sensing
- Application of Remote Sensing to land area
- Weather Forecasting
- Ensemble Weather Forecasting
- Generative Models applied to Weather Forecasting
- Reinforement Learning
- Deep Reinforcement Learning
- Deep Learning applied to Robotics
